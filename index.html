I'm Melvin, a software developer. I like problems that fit on one screen and tools that stay out of the way.

Most of my work is the unglamorous kind: the form that submits, the list that loads fast, the message that actually helps when something breaks.

I care about small, honest details: the spacing, the empty state, the moment after the click.

If that sounds like the way you like to build, I'd like to hear what you're working on.
